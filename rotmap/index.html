Ravages Hex Map

Draw

Click to add snapped points (corners, edge midpoints, centers, sub-points, and the vertices of features you've already drawn — so new lines connect precisely) — at most one hex ahead per click, so nothing gets skipped. Enter/double-click finishes, Esc cancels, Ctrl+Z undoes. Hold Alt for free placement, or E to snap anywhere along the nearest hex edge (not just corners/midpoints). Coast: locked to a single hex — the first click picks the hex, and points snap only to that hex's corners/edges/centre/sub-points (hold E to snap anywhere along an edge). Points also snap back onto the line you're drawing: once you have three, the starting node turns into a teal ring, and clicking it closes the loop and finishes the line — that's how you draw an island smaller than a hex. Then click the side that is sea. Coast lines act as barriers, so two coasts can enclose an inner sea. Works on land and sea hexes. Shift+click a coast later to re-pick its sea side. Erase: click a node to remove just it, click between nodes to cut that segment (splits the line), Shift+click to remove a whole line, or drag across the map to wipe whole features/strongholds as you go; clicking a stronghold marker removes it — including datasheet strongholds (they're hidden persistently; Ctrl+Z or the Stronghold tool brings them back). Stronghold: click to place/restore the marker exactly within its hex, Shift+click toggles coastal/port (blue ring = port, where embark/disembark is possible). Sea- and river-side strongholds are ports by default; Shift+click overrides that either way for the exceptions. Label: click any hex to name or rename it (works on datasheet strongholds too; clear the text to reset to the datasheet name).

Autosaved locally.

Routes

Click hexes to add waypoints to the active route. Right-click — or Remove last, for touchscreens, where there is no right button — removes the last one.

Isochrone

Travel-time shading from an origin hex. Army uses the movement rules above; Message / Rumour spread in a straight line (as the crow flies), ignoring terrain, at 240 / 90 miles per IRL day. Shift+click the map (or press Set origin, then click).
